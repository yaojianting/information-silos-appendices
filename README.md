## Contents

Files: `Appendices_JCEM.pdf` (for reading in the browser) and `Appendices_JCEM.docx` (editable source).

- **Appendix B.** Regulations and standards referenced, with abbreviations and original Chinese titles.
- **Appendix C.** IDEF0 functional models of the safety information chain in construction during airport operations (Figs. C1 to C4).
