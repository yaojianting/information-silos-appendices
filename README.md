# Appendices to "No actor is an island? Uncovering the information silo effect and its impact on safety network resilience"

Appendices S1–S3 cited in the Supplemental Material section of the manuscript submitted to the *Journal of Construction Engineering and Management* (ASCE).

**Authors:** Jianting Yao (School of Public Affairs, Zhejiang University; Department of the Built Environment, National University of Singapore) and Yang Miang Goh (Department of the Built Environment, National University of Singapore)

## Contents

Files: `Appendices_JCEM.pdf` (for reading in the browser) and `Appendices_JCEM.docx` (editable source).

- **Appendix S1.** Safety information exchange among the 14 information subjects in civil aviation construction (Table S1).
- **Appendix S2.** Regulations and standards referenced, with abbreviations and original Chinese titles.
- **Appendix S3.** IDEF0 functional models of the safety information chain in construction during airport operations (Figs. S1–S4).

Figs. S1–S4 of Appendix S3 are also provided separately for easier viewing: `Figures_S1-S4.pdf` (one figure per page with its caption), `Figures_S1-S4.docx` (the same pages in Word) and `Fig_S1.png` to `Fig_S4.png` (image files).
